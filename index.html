Unitopia

the Island of Magic and Virgin Margarita's

On our tropical island we offer otherwordly experiances, your dream is Unitopia's reality. Unitopia is located in between Hawaii and the South Pole and has the inexpensive flight fee of 13$ in which you ride on the back of our very own Narwhals.

On the Island, take a tour of the 27 natural waterfalls and hotsprings available. Go berry picking with the Unicorn locals or take a swim off the coast with the equatic life. At Unitopia we pride ourselves for our good communication with the underwater citzens including mermaids, selkies, and Krakens.

At Unitopia you might get a chance to meet public figures such as Buttercup the Unicorn, the Mayor of the Island and creator of the restort. Buttercup's family have lived off the land for the last millenium and have connected many previously at war communities such as the fairy's and the elves.

At Unitopia we offer a variety of meal options amongst your wildest dream's including the national drink of the Island, Margarita's, and the national dish Mango sticky Rice. The favorite fruit of the island is Mango, used with other fruits on the island such as fire berries and spiced nectar. The most signature drink to the island is a Mango Margarita, usually served along with rainbow oysters.
